5. Gradient System

Don't overuse gradients. Use them primarily for hero accents and important CTAs.

Creative gradient

background: linear-gradient(135deg, #3B82F6, #7C3AED);

That little violet addition can give your UI/UX side more personality without making violet part of your main brand.

Subtle background glow

background: radial-gradient(

    rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.15),

);
